# METH-001: 不可逆 10 节点认知棘轮 / Irreversible 10-Node Cognitive Ratchet

## 纯粹的数学边界 / A Pure Mathematical Boundary
> **[CN]:** Axiom-0 的核心不涉及任何特定的概率计算逻辑，而是专注于拓扑约束。我们通过 10 个节点构建了一个被称为“认知棘轮 (Cognitive Ratchet)”的单向信息流通道。信息如同穿过只能单向转动的机械齿轮，绝无后退的可能。
> **[EN]:** The core of Axiom-0 does not concern itself with specific probabilistic compute logic, but focuses purely on topological constraints. Through 10 nodes, we construct a one-way information channel known as the "Cognitive Ratchet." Information passes through like mechanical gears that only turn in one direction, leaving absolutely no possibility of regression.

## 彻底斩断重试环 / Severing the Retry Loop
> **[CN]:** 高维信息在流经脱水 (Dehydration)、抽象 (Abstraction)、收束 (Convergence) 节点的每一个缝隙时，都会接受极其冷酷的结构检查。任何多余的熵、任何未经授权的自洽推理，都会触发立刻熔断。
> **[EN]:** As high-dimensional information flows through every crevice of the Dehydration, Abstraction, and Convergence nodes, it undergoes ruthlessly cold structural checks. Any superfluous entropy or unauthorized self-consistent reasoning instantly triggers a physical meltdown.
