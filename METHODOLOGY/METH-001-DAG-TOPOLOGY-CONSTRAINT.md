# METH-001: 不可逆 10 节点认知棘轮 / Irreversible 10-Node Cognitive Ratchet

## 纯粹的数学边界 / A Pure Mathematical Boundary
> **[CN]:** Axiom-0 的核心不涉及任何特定的概率计算逻辑，也不依赖任何大语言模型内部的黑盒涌现能力。它完全专注于利用坚不可摧的纯数学边界实施拓扑约束。我们通过 10 个被严格定义的物理节点，构建了一个被称为“认知棘轮 (Cognitive Ratchet)”的绝对单向信息流通道。信息在系统中的每一次状态转移，都如同穿过只能单向转动的精钢机械齿轮，只能被推向下一个绝对确定的阶段，绝无任何向后回退的物理可能。
> **[EN]:** The core of Axiom-0 does not concern itself with any specific probabilistic compute logic, nor does it rely on the black-box emergent capabilities inside any large language model. It focuses purely on enforcing topological constraints using impenetrable, pure mathematical boundaries. Through 10 strictly defined physical nodes, we construct an absolute one-way information flow channel known as the "Cognitive Ratchet." Every state transition of information within the system is akin to passing through solid steel mechanical gears that can only turn in one direction—it is forcefully pushed to the next absolutely deterministic stage, with absolutely zero physical possibility of regressing backward.

## 彻底斩断重试环 / Severing the Retry Loop
> **[CN]:** 高维度的非结构化信息在流经脱水 (Dehydration)、抽象 (Abstraction) 以及收束 (Convergence) 节点的每一个缝隙时，都会被迫接受极其冷酷、无情的代数结构检查。系统剥夺了智能体自我解释的权力，代之以基于哈希校验和模式匹配的铁律。任何多余的熵增、任何未经底层协议授权的自洽推理论证，都会在瞬间触发核心的物理熔断指令。重试环在这里不仅被废除，更是被视为对系统纯洁性的严重物理污染。
> **[EN]:** As high-dimensional, unstructured information flows through every minute crevice of the Dehydration, Abstraction, and Convergence nodes, it is forced to undergo ruthlessly cold and unforgiving algebraic structural checks. The system strips the agent of its power to self-explain, replacing it with the iron laws of hash validation and pattern matching. Any superfluous entropy increase, or any unauthorized self-consistent deductive reasoning not sanctioned by the underlying protocol, instantly triggers the core's physical meltdown command. Here, the retry loop is not merely abolished; it is regarded as a severe physical contamination against the purity of the system.
