# METH-008: 合成内容排斥边界 / Synthetic Content Exclusion Boundary

## 防御性认知吞噬 / Defensive Cognitive Ingestion
> **[CN]:** 当外部环境（如社区平台）开始排斥 AI，Axiom-0 必须同样内化这种排斥逻辑，形成系统的“防御性认知吞噬”。任何流入系统的数据流，如果其特征向量显示出高度的合成概率（缺乏人类特有的逻辑跳跃或粗糙的真实感），都将在脱水前置阶段被无情剔除。
> **[EN]:** When the external environment (such as community platforms) begins to reject AI, Axiom-0 must equally internalize this rejection logic, forming a systemic "defensive cognitive ingestion." Any data stream flowing into the system, if its feature vectors indicate a high probability of synthesis (lacking the logical leaps or the rough realism unique to humans), will be ruthlessly eliminated in the pre-dehydration stage.

## 协议层面的物理红线 / Physical Red Lines at the Protocol Level
> **[CN]:** 这条边界不是建议，而是物理红线。它确保 Axiom-0 的知识库和方法论不会被同质化的概率废话稀释。只有通过了严格人工介入痕迹验证的输入，才能获准进入下一级抽象节点。
> **[EN]:** This boundary is not a suggestion; it is a physical red line. It ensures that Axiom-0's knowledge base and methodologies are not diluted by homogenized probabilistic nonsense. Only inputs that have passed strict verification of human intervention traces are granted permission to enter the next level of abstraction nodes.
