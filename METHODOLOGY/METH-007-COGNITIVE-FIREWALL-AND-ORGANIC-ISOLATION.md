# METH-007: 认知防火墙与有机隔离 / Cognitive Firewall and Organic Isolation

## 纯人类网络边界的回归 / Return of the Pure Human Network Boundary
> **[CN]:** 随着大模型生成内容的泛滥，高价值的知识网络（如 Hacker News）正在从协议层面部署针对 AI 生成文本的零容忍封锁。这证明了在高度熵增的信息海洋中，只有建立冷酷的“认知防火墙”，才能保护有机讨论（人类的原始智慧与思辨）免受概率引擎污染。Axiom-0 的物理隔离思想正源于这种极端的自救措施。
> **[EN]:** With the proliferation of LLM-generated content, high-value knowledge networks (e.g., Hacker News) are deploying zero-tolerance blockades against AI-generated text at the protocol level. This proves that in an ocean of highly increasing entropy, only by establishing a cold "cognitive firewall" can organic discussion (human raw wisdom and speculation) be protected from probabilistic engine pollution. Axiom-0's philosophy of physical isolation originates precisely from this extreme self-preservation measure.

## 系统级熵减与反向蒸馏 / Systemic Entropy Reduction and Reverse Distillation
> **[CN]:** 我们不再盲目摄取一切合成内容，而是通过反向蒸馏法则，过滤掉冗余的生成修辞，仅保留人类干预的锚点数据。系统在 A1 考古节点就将这类带有明确拒绝态度的信息定义为高价值（`[REAL]`），以此作为校准自身零熵协议的参照物。
> **[EN]:** We no longer blindly ingest all synthetic content; instead, through the reverse distillation law, we filter out redundant generative rhetoric, retaining only the anchor data of human intervention. At the A1 archaeology node, the system defines such information carrying a clear rejection attitude as high-value (`[REAL]`), using it as a reference to calibrate its own zero-entropy protocol.
