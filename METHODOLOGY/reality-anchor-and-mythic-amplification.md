# Reality Anchor and Mythic Amplification

## Three-Layer Model

### Layer 1  Reality Anchor
> **[CN]**: 真实协议 真实模型 真实仓库 真实论文 真实命令
> **[EN]**: Real protocol Real model Real warehouse Real paper Real command
> **[CN]**: 它们提供方向与牵引力
> **[EN]**: They provide direction and traction

### Layer 2  Mythic Amplification
> **[CN]**: 把现实锚点提升为更高密度的架构语言
> **[EN]**: Promote reality anchors to higher-density architectural languages
> **[CN]**: 例如 节拍化 命名化 契约化 流程化
> **[EN]**: For example, rhythmization, naming, contracting, and processization

### Layer 3  Postprocessing
> **[CN]**: 将喷流内容重新切分为
> **[EN]**: Re-segment the jet content into

- **[CN]**: 可执行
  - **[EN]**: Executable
- **[CN]**: 可验证
  - **[EN]**: Verifiable
- **[CN]**: 可保留为原创概念
  - **[EN]**: Can be retained as an original concept
- **[CN]**: 必须标为包装
  - **[EN]**: Must be marked as packaging

## Benefits

- **[CN]**: 不会陷入空想式发散
  - **[EN]**: Will not fall into utopian divergence
- **[CN]**: 也不会被写实主义束缚住灵感
  - **[EN]**: Nor will inspiration be constrained by realism
- **[CN]**: 允许项目在现实与想象之间形成高压通道
  - **[EN]**: Allows projects to create a high-voltage channel between reality and imagination

## Rule

> **[CN]**: 先有锚点
> **[EN]**: Anchor first
> **[CN]**: 再做增压
> **[EN]**: Do supercharging again
> **[CN]**: 最后再脱水
> **[EN]**: Finally, dehydrate

## 0-Entropy Constraints
> **[CN]**: 现实锚点的选取标准必须满足零熵（0-Entropy）原则，即无冗余、无黑盒。
> **[EN]**: The selection criteria for reality anchors must strictly adhere to the Zero-Entropy (0-Entropy) principle: no redundancy, no opacity.
