# 零熵原则：Axiom-0 设计哲学 / Zero-Entropy Principles: Axiom-0 Philosophy

---

## 概述 / Overview
> **[CN]**: “少即是多”。Axiom-0 的核心不是无止境地堆功能，而是通过极简的协议（ZECP）实现极高的智慧。零熵意味着没有任何废话、没有任何黑盒、没有任何冗余。
> 
> **[EN]**: "Less is Intelligence." Axiom-0 is predicated on the Zero-Entropy Hypothesis—that system performance is inversely proportional to architectural complexity. By enforcing the ZECP constraints, we achieve 1.0 Physics Recall through minimized logic-drift and maximized information density.

---

## 三大核心支柱 / The 3 Pillars

### 1. 零依赖 (Zero-Dependency)
- **[CN]**: 禁止乱加包。只允许使用最底层的 Python 库，保证每一行代码都可审。
- **[EN]**: Elimination of external black-box substrates. Only foundational, auditable primitives are permitted within the Axiom-0 core.

### 2. 零冗余 (Zero-Redundancy)
- **[CN]**: 抹杀废代码。任何三天没用过的节点或三分钟不被调用的逻辑，都要被剪掉。
- **[EN]**: Aggressive logic pruning. Inactive synapses and dead-code branches are autonomously purged via the Node 08 (Pruning) cycle.

### 3. 零黑盒 (Zero-Opacity)
- **[CN]**: 彻底透明。AI 处理的每一步都必须有签名、有哈希、可回溯。
- **[EN]**: Full cryptographic auditability. Every cognitive state transition is hashed, signed (ADR-001), and traceable within the Axiom-0 continuum.

---

## 零熵方程 / The Entropy Equation
> **[CN]**: 系统的性能由信息密度和协同效率决定。
> **[EN]**: $Performance \propto \frac{Information\_Density}{System\_Entropy}$
> Axiom-0 targets an entropy level of $< 0.1$ for all production-ready artifacts.
