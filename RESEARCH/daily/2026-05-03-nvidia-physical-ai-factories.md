## 1. 发生了什么 (What)

### 1.1 物理AI与仿真 (Physical AI and Simulation)
> **[CN]**: NVIDIA、Google DeepMind和Disney Research共同开发了开源物理引擎Newton，依托NVIDIA Warp构建，为机器人仿真和学习提供精确和可扩展的基础。物理AI的重点在于将智能体推进到现实世界。
>
> **[EN]**: NVIDIA, Google DeepMind, and Disney Research jointly developed the open-source physics engine Newton, built on NVIDIA Warp, providing an accurate and scalable foundation for robotic simulation and learning. The focus of physical AI is to propel agents into the real world.

### 1.2 800V HVDC AI工厂架构 (800V HVDC AI Factory Architecture)
> **[CN]**: 随着AI机架进入兆瓦级规模，NVIDIA及其行业合作伙伴正在推进800V直流电源架构，为未来的数据中心提供更高的效率、可扩展性和可靠性。
>
> **[EN]**: As AI racks move to megawatt scale, NVIDIA and industry partners are advancing an 800V DC power architecture to deliver higher efficiency, scalability, and reliability for future data centers.

## 2. 为什么重要 (Why)

### 2.1 算力与能耗的物理极限 (Physical Limits of Compute and Power)
> **[CN]**: AI智能体的复杂性和模型的扩展带来了呈指数级增长的能耗需求。800V直流架构不仅仅是电源改进，更是大规模智能体群体部署的物理前提。
>
> **[EN]**: The complexity of AI agents and the scaling of models bring exponentially growing power consumption demands. The 800V DC architecture is not just a power supply improvement; it is the physical prerequisite for the large-scale deployment of agent swarms.

### 2.2 软硬件的边界模糊 (Blurring Boundaries Between Hardware and Software)
> **[CN]**: 诸如使用DeepSeek-R1配合推理期扩展（Test-Time Compute）来自动生成GPU算子的实践，表明计算基础设施正在被AI自身重塑，形成了底层的闭环。
>
> **[EN]**: Practices such as using DeepSeek-R1 combined with inference-time scaling (Test-Time Compute) to automatically generate GPU kernels show that the compute infrastructure is being reshaped by AI itself, forming a low-level closed loop.

## 3. Axiom-0 脱水 (Axiom-0 Dehydration)

### 3.1 物理沙盒的绝对锚定 (Absolute Grounding of the Physical Sandbox)
> **[CN]**: Axiom-0 将大模型视为“高危的概率外包组件”。无论NVIDIA的底层算力多强大，在我们的10节点有向无环图中，这些算力输出都只能作为T-07节点（Sandbox Exe）中受严格控制的强类型执行。兆瓦级的机架算力必须被截断、隔离，并服务于绝对的确定性。
>
> **[EN]**: Axiom-0 treats LLMs as "high-risk probabilistic outsourced components." Regardless of the immense power of NVIDIA's underlying compute, in our 10-node DAG, these compute outputs can only serve as strictly controlled, strong-type executions in the T-07 node (Sandbox Exe). Megawatt-scale rack compute must be truncated, isolated, and serve absolute determinism.

### 3.2 算力降级与零熵转化 (Compute Downgrade and Zero-Entropy Conversion)
> **[CN]**: 物理AI（Physical AI）试图将高熵的神经网络映射到现实物理世界。Axiom-0的“脱水”过程反其道而行之：我们拒绝直接涌现，任何由庞大算力产生的复杂策略，都必须经过T-04（Morphing）的液态路由，最终退化为基础的Python或SQLite无锁队列操作。
>
> **[EN]**: Physical AI attempts to map high-entropy neural networks to the real physical world. Axiom-0's "dehydration" process does the exact opposite: we reject direct emergence, and any complex strategy generated by massive compute must pass through T-04's (Morphing) liquid routing, ultimately degrading into fundamental Python or SQLite lock-free queue operations.
