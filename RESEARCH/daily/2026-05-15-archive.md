>
> **[EN]:** Why take this path? As early API wrapper architectures collapsed in enterprise scenarios, developers realized that simple Prompts could not control complex external system interactions. Nous Research hoped to burn the "how-to-use-tools" knowledge directly into the model weights to reduce context burden.
