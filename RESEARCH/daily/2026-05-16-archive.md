>
> **[EN]:** Why release Cosmos? NVIDIA astutely recognized that relying solely on the pure-text capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs) was inadequate for handling "Physical AI" with its complex spatial and temporal dynamics. Acquiring large-scale, high-quality embodied AI training data in the real world is prohibitively expensive. NVIDIA aimed to construct a model that understands not just language, but "physical laws." By combining virtual and physical realities (Digital Twin), they intended to enable the generation of massive, controllable environments during model training, solving the critical data bottleneck from perception to planning.
