## 1. 做了什么：专业智能体的协作 / What Was Done: Collaboration of Specialized Agents

> **[CN]**: 2025 年的企业级 AI 框架大量采用多智能体架构。例如在保险理赔中，使用七个专职智能体（包括 Planner Agent 和 Cyber Agent）来共同处理单个索赔。
>
> **[EN]**: Enterprise AI frameworks in 2025 heavily adopted multi-agent architectures. For example, in insurance claims, seven specialized agents (including a Planner Agent and a Cyber Agent) were used to jointly process a single claim.

### 1.1 动态协作网络 (Dynamic Collaboration Network)
- **概念 (Concept)**:
  > **[CN]**: 智能体被赋予了复杂的通信协议和规划能力，允许它们自主协商、交换信息并得出结论。这种模式强调智能体的“主动性”和“自主性”。
  > **[EN]**: Agents were given complex communication protocols and planning capabilities, allowing them to autonomously negotiate, exchange information, and reach conclusions. This model emphasized the "proactivity" and "autonomy" of agents.

## 2. 为什么做：追求复杂任务的自动化 / Why It Was Done: Pursuing Automation of Complex Tasks

> **[CN]**: 设计这些系统的初衷是为了应对单一大型语言模型在处理多步骤、高复杂度任务时的局限性。通过将任务拆分为特定领域的子任务，业界试图提高系统的准确率和效率。
>
> **[EN]**: The original intent behind designing these systems was to address the limitations of a single large language model when handling multi-step, highly complex tasks. By breaking tasks into domain-specific subtasks, the industry attempted to improve system accuracy and efficiency.

- **解决单一模型的幻觉 (Solving Single-Model Hallucinations)**:
  > **[CN]**: 业界认为不同角色的相互监督可以减少错误，缩短处理时间。
  > **[EN]**: The industry believed that mutual supervision among different roles could reduce errors and shorten processing time.

## 3. Axiom-0 降维脱水与碾压 / Zero-Entropy Dehydration and Axiom-0 Crushing

> **[CN]**: 尽管多智能体系统在表面上提高了效率，但其本质上是构建了一个不可靠的概率对话网络。当多个概率引擎相互交谈时，熵增是不可避免的。Axiom-0 认为，系统架构绝不能依赖于 LLM 之间的不可预见交互。
>
> **[EN]**: Although multi-agent systems superficially improved efficiency, they fundamentally built an unreliable probabilistic dialogue network. When multiple probabilistic engines talk to each other, entropy increase is inevitable. Axiom-0 believes that system architecture must never rely on unforeseen interactions between LLMs.

- **将协作脱水为流水线 (Dehydrating Collaboration into a Pipeline)**:
  > **[CN]**: XCube Labs 报告中的多智能体协作网络在 Axiom-0 中被无情地脱水。我们废除智能体间的自由对话，代之以 10 节点认知连续体（10-Node Cognitive Continuum）。一切处理流程必须是单向的、基于状态机的操作，每个节点的输出都经过严格校验并锁定为 SQLite 中的不可变记录。没有协商，只有执行。
  > **[EN]**: The multi-agent collaboration network in the XCube Labs report is ruthlessly dehydrated in Axiom-0. We abolish free dialogue between agents and replace it with the 10-Node Cognitive Continuum. All processing workflows must be unidirectional, state-machine-based operations, where the output of each node is strictly verified and locked as immutable records in SQLite. There is no negotiation, only execution.
