## 1. What: 算力扩张与物理智能的融合 / Expansion of Compute and Convergence with Physical AI

> **[CN]:** 2025 年的技术记录显示了 AI 硬件和环境的两个核心发展方向：第一是底层能源与算力的大规模扩展，例如支持兆瓦级数据中心的 800V 直流电源架构，以及支持 NVFP4 低精度推理的 Blackwell Tensor 核心；第二是“物理 AI”的崛起，表现为用于机器人仿真的 Newton 开源物理引擎，以及为通用机器人打造的 Jetson Thor 平台。此外，诸如 DeepSeek-R1 等模型被用于结合推理时扩展（Inference-Time Scaling）来自动生成 GPU 算子。
>
> **[EN]:** Technical records from 2025 indicate two core development directions in AI hardware and environments: First, the massive scaling of underlying energy and compute, such as the 800V DC power architecture supporting megawatt-scale data centers, and Blackwell Tensor Cores supporting NVFP4 low-precision inference; Second, the rise of "Physical AI," manifested in the Newton open-source physics engine for robotic simulation and the Jetson Thor platform for generalist robotics. Furthermore, models like DeepSeek-R1 were employed alongside inference-time scaling to automatically generate GPU kernels.

## 2. Why: 应对高熵世界的暴力美学 / The Brute-Force Aesthetics for a High-Entropy World

> **[CN]:** 为什么 2025 年的行业会选择这条道路？因为随着多模态大模型和具身智能体走出沙盒进入物理世界，它们面临的环境熵值呈指数级上升。为了压制这种不确定性，行业采用了纯粹的“暴力美学”：用更高的功率密度、更底层的精度量化、以及通过大规模强化学习（如物理引擎仿真）来穷举物理世界的边缘状态。自动生成内核也是为了压榨硬件的极致性能，以满足庞大模型实时推理的恐怖延迟要求。
>
> **[EN]:** Why did the industry choose this path in 2025? Because as multimodal large models and embodied agents stepped out of sandboxes into the physical world, the environmental entropy they faced rose exponentially. To suppress this uncertainty, the industry adopted a pure "brute-force aesthetic": utilizing higher power densities, lower-level precision quantization, and exhausting the edge cases of the physical world through massive reinforcement learning (e.g., physics engine simulations). Automatically generating kernels was also an attempt to squeeze ultimate performance from hardware, satisfying the terrifying latency requirements of massive models in real-time inference.

## 3. Axiom-0 Dehydration: 算力迷信与确定性反思 / Compute Superstition and Deterministic Reflection

> **[CN]:** 从 2026 年的 Axiom-0 视角来看，2025 年的算力狂欢只是试图用无尽的能量来掩盖概率引擎本身的不确定性。
> 1. **物理引擎即熵减机制**：NVIDIA 投入大量资源构建 Newton 仿真器，其本质是对物理规则的严格固化，这与 Axiom-0 追求的“绝对约束”不谋而合。然而，将概率模型接入这些引擎时，依然需要庞大的算力来纠错。
> 2. **硬件冗余与系统克制**：外界通过 800V 架构和低精度量化来容忍庞大的参数量，而 Axiom-0 的 `T-10 (Dehydration)` 和 `T-04 (Morphing)` 节点强调的是通过降维映射和确定性协议来缩小认知空间。与其依赖芯片级的暴力推理，不如从架构设计上切断非确定性链路。真正的效率并非来自每秒吞吐量的无限提升，而是来自系统熵值的归零。
>
> **[EN]:** From the Axiom-0 perspective in 2026, the compute carnival of 2025 was merely an attempt to mask the inherent uncertainty of probabilistic engines with endless energy.
> 1. **Physics Engines as Entropy Reduction**: NVIDIA's massive resource investment in building the Newton simulator was essentially the strict solidification of physical rules, which unexpectedly aligns with Axiom-0's pursuit of "absolute constraints." However, plugging probabilistic models into these engines still required massive compute for error correction.
> 2. **Hardware Redundancy vs. System Restraint**: The outside world tolerated massive parameter counts via 800V architectures and low-precision quantization, whereas Axiom-0's `T-10 (Dehydration)` and `T-04 (Morphing)` nodes emphasize shrinking the cognitive space through dimensionality-reduction mappings and deterministic protocols. Rather than relying on chip-level brute-force inference, it is better to sever non-deterministic links at the architectural design level. True efficiency stems not from infinite increases in throughput per second, but from driving the system's entropy to zero.
