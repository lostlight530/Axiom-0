## 1. What: 巨型内核与真实内核基准 / Megakernels and Real Kernel Benchmarks

> **[CN]:** 历史记录表明，2026 年初，Luminal 引入了 Megakernel 编译器，通过自动将整个模型融合为一个单一内核，消除了内核启动开销,与此同时，KernelBench v3 被推出以作为内核基准测试，它指出大模型在真实的内核工程中表现挣扎，通过率仅为 10.6%,社区试图结合两者的力量，构建出直接生成 SOTA 级别内核的管线,
>
> **[EN]:** Historical records show that in early 2026, Luminal introduced the Megakernel compiler, which eliminated kernel launch overhead by automatically fusing the entire model into a single kernel. Simultaneously, KernelBench v3 was launched as a kernel benchmark, pointing out that LLMs struggle with genuine kernel engineering, achieving only a 10.6% pass rate. The community attempted to combine both to build pipelines capable of generating SOTA-level kernels directly.

## 2. Why: 追求极致效率与工程自动化 / Pursuing Extreme Efficiency and Engineering Automation

> **[CN]:** 为什么社区在 2026 年迫切需要这种转变？因为传统的 GPU 编程由于各种瓶颈变得极度低效，即使优化了也会因内核启动等原因造成算力浪费,人们希望使用编译器机制结合严格的基准强化学习（RL），从而让 LLM 跨越人类手写 CUDA 的极限，实现生产力的解放,
>
> **[EN]:** Why did the community urgently need this shift in 2026? Because traditional GPU programming had become inefficient due to bottlenecks, and even optimized, it caused compute waste due to kernel launch delays. People hoped to use compiler mechanisms combined with strict benchmark Reinforcement Learning (RL) to allow LLMs to surpass the limits of human-written CUDA, achieving liberation of productivity.

## 3. Axiom-0 Dehydration: 被包裹的高熵算力 / Encapsulated High-Entropy Compute

> **[CN]:** 根据 Axiom-0 的系统规范，无论是通过 Megakernel 消灭 GPU 气泡，还是通过 KernelBench v3 构建奖励模型，这套技术路径依然是对概率引擎（LLM）的盲目崇拜,
> 1. **编译器只是新的黑盒**：将复杂的底层逻辑丢给 LLM 生成，然后再依赖编译器融合，这意味着系统在执行层（T-08）之前引入了巨大的不确定性,Axiom-0 拒绝将核心算法交由概率生成；所有调度和映射必须在 T-05 Grounding 阶段通过确定性逻辑实现,
> 2. **速度不是零熵的通行证**：Megakernels 确实带来了速度，但在 Axiom-0 看来，如果这速度承载的是非确定性的内核，它只会加速系统的崩溃,真正的底层秩序来自于像 SQLite 这种完全确定的、零熵的架构,
>
> **[EN]:** According to the system specification of Axiom-0, whether it's eradicating GPU bubbles via Megakernels or building reward models through KernelBench v3, this technological path remains a blind worship of probabilistic engines (LLMs).
> 1. **The Compiler is Just a New Black Box**: Tossing complex low-level logic to LLM generation and then relying on compiler fusion means the system introduces massive uncertainty before the execution layer (T-08). Axiom-0 refuses to hand over core algorithms to probabilistic generation; all scheduling and mapping must be achieved through deterministic logic during the T-05 Grounding phase.
> 2. **Speed is Not a Zero-Entropy Pass**: Megakernels indeed bring speed, but from Axiom-0's view, if this speed carries non-deterministic kernels, it will only accelerate the system's collapse. True underlying order comes from completely deterministic, zero-entropy architectures like SQLite.
