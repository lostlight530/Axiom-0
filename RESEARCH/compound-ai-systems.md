# 复合 AI 系统：Axiom-0 架构研究 / Compound AI Systems: Axiom-0 Research

## 摘要 / Abstract
> **[CN]**: “三个臭皮匠，顶个诸葛亮”。这份报告研究了如何把多个 AI 组合起来，让它们干出比单个大模型厉害得多的活计。
> 
> **[EN]**: This research validates the "Compound AI Hypothesis"—that orchestrated ensembles of specialized models consistently outperform monolithic systems. The Axiom-0 continuum provides the implementation framework (ZECP) for these architectural findings.

---

## 1. 复合 AI 假设 / The Hypothesis
> **[CN]**: 当每个零件都各司其职，而不是互相打架的时候，整体的力量就会爆发。
> 
> **[EN]**: Systemic emergence occurs when components are designed for complementarity. Individual model limitations are mitigated through multi-agent collaboration and Axiom-0 verification protocols.

### 1.1 核心原则 (Principles)
- **专业化 (Specialization)**: 每个 AI 只干自己最擅长的活儿。 / Domain-specific agent roles.
- **组合性 (Composition)**: 像搭积木一样把 AI 拼起来。 / 10-node recursive continuum.
- **自愈性 (Resilience)**: 哪怕坏了一个 AI，系统也能自己修好。 / Self-healing via HASH protocol.

---

## 2. 架构模式 / Architectural Patterns
- **路由模式 (Router)**: 像前台接待一样，根据客人的需求分发给专门的部门。 / Task distribution via capability matching.
- **集成模式 (Ensemble)**: 投票制，大家一起审稿，选出最好的那个结果。 / Consensus-based aggregation for robustness.
- **流水线模式 (Pipeline)**: 第一步干完给第二步，环环相扣。 / Sequential stage-based processing chains.
