## 1. 做了什么：复合 AI 系统的核心架构 / What Was Done: Core Architectures of Compound AI Systems

> **[CN]**: 2025 年的业界普遍认识到，系统层面的突现能力（Systemic Emergence）远胜于单一模型的算力堆叠。复合 AI 系统主要采用了三种架构模式：路由（Router）、集成（Ensemble）与流水线（Pipeline）。
>
> **[EN]**: The industry in 2025 universally recognized that systemic emergence far outpaces the scaling of monolithic models. Compound AI Systems primarily adopted three architectural patterns: Router, Ensemble, and Pipeline.

- **路由模式 (Router)**:
  > **[CN]**: 利用轻量级分类器将不同复杂度的任务分发给专门的模型或工具。
  > **[EN]**: Utilizing lightweight classifiers to distribute tasks of varying complexity to specialized models or tools.
- **集成模式 (Ensemble)**:
  > **[CN]**: 多个模型并行处理同一任务，通过投票或共识机制（如 CoMCTS）来降低个体的幻觉率。
  > **[EN]**: Multiple models processing the same task in parallel, using voting or consensus mechanisms (e.g., CoMCTS) to reduce individual hallucination rates.
- **流水线模式 (Pipeline)**:
  > **[CN]**: 将复杂任务拆解为序列化的步骤（感知、规划、检索、执行），每个步骤由专门的 Agent 负责。
  > **[EN]**: Deconstructing complex tasks into serialized steps (perception, planning, retrieval, execution), each handled by specialized agents.

## 2. 为什么做：突破概率坍塌的极限 / Why It Was Done: Breaking the Limits of Probabilistic Collapse

> **[CN]**: 单体大模型在处理复杂、多步推理任务时，面临着不可逾越的“概率坍塌”问题。复合系统试图解决这一痛点：
> 
> **[EN]**: Monolithic LLMs face insurmountable "probabilistic collapse" when handling complex, multi-step reasoning tasks. Compound systems attempt to solve this pain point:

- **缓解幻觉叠加 (Mitigating Hallucination Compounding)**:
  > **[CN]**: 单一模型的错误会在长上下文中指数级放大。复合系统通过在节点间引入验证（如集成投票），试图阻断错误的传播。
  > **[EN]**: Errors in a single model amplify exponentially over long contexts. Compound systems try to block error propagation by introducing inter-node verification (e.g., ensemble voting).
- **专业化效率 (Specialization Efficiency)**:
  > **[CN]**: 并非所有任务都需要千亿参数的算力。复合系统允许在简单计算上使用小模型，在复杂推理上调用大模型，从而平衡了延迟与成本。
  > **[EN]**: Not all tasks require 100B+ parameter compute. Compound systems allow for small models on simple calculations and large models for complex reasoning, balancing latency and cost.

> **[CN]**: 尽管 2025 年的复合系统方向正确，但它们大多数仍在使用大模型去编排大模型，本质上依然是一个高熵的黑盒网络。Axiom-0 对此进行了冷酷的脱水重构。
>
> **[EN]**: Although the direction of compound systems in 2025 was correct, most still used LLMs to orchestrate LLMs, essentially remaining a high-entropy black-box network. Axiom-0 performed a brutal dehydration and reconstruction of this.

- **从概率流水线到 10 节点连续体 (From Probabilistic Pipelines to the 10-Node Continuum)**:
  > **[CN]**: Axiom-0 废除了基于 LLM 的不确定流水线，将其固化为严格定义的 T-01 到 T-10 单向认知流。每一个步骤交接都必须通过具备密码学签名的状态验证，不允许模型自主决定流程分支。
  > **[EN]**: Axiom-0 abolished LLM-based uncertain pipelines, solidifying them into a strictly defined T-01 to T-10 unidirectional cognitive flow. Every step handoff must pass cryptographically signed state verification; models are not allowed to autonomously decide flow branches.

- **从松散路由到液态结构变形 (From Loose Routing to Liquid Structural Morphing)**:
  > **[CN]**: 传统的路由机制依赖模型的临时判断。Axiom-0 通过 `T-04 (Morphing)` 节点，基于系统负载和客观计算散度（KL-Divergence），强行在物理层级进行状态切换（Solid -> Liquid），以无锁队列驱动多个执行线程，彻底消除了路由过程中的概率抖动。
  > **[EN]**: Traditional routing mechanisms rely on temporary model judgments. Axiom-0, via the `T-04 (Morphing)` node, forcefully shifts states (Solid -> Liquid) at the physical level based on system load and objective computational divergence (KL-Divergence), driving multiple execution threads via lock-free queues, completely eliminating probabilistic jitter during routing.
