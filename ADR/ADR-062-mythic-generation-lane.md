# ADR-062 Mythic Generation Lane

## 状态 / Status
**已采纳 (Accepted)**

- **[CN]**: 日期：2026-04-18
  - **[EN]**: Date: 2026-04-18
- **[CN]**: 决策者：lostlight530 + AI副驾驶
  - **[EN]**: Deciders: lostlight530 + AI copilot
- **[CN]**: 范围：Axiom-0 方法论和上游构思
  - **[EN]**: Scope: Axiom-0 methodology and upstream ideation

## 背景 / Context

> **[CN]**: Axiom-0 的上游并不是传统严谨情报系统
> **[EN]**: The upstream of Axiom-0 is not a traditional rigorous intelligence system
> **[CN]**: 它需要一个高压 高频 高密度的灵感喷流环境
> **[EN]**: It requires a high-pressure, high-frequency, high-density inspiration jet environment
> **[CN]**: 当前 Gemini 自动化流程的真实目标 是以现实技术脉络为燃料 生成可命名 可结构化 可继续演化的架构神话
> **[EN]**: The real goal of the current Gemini automation process is to use real technology context as fuel to generate an architectural myth that can be named, structured, and can continue to evolve.

## 决策 / Decision

> **[CN]**: 正式承认 `Mythic Generation Lane` 为 Axiom-0 的合法上游通道
> **[EN]**: Officially recognize `Mythic Generation Lane` as the legal upstream channel of Axiom-0
> **[CN]**: 该通道负责生成
> **[EN]**: This channel is responsible for generating

- **[CN]**: 新命名
  - **[EN]**: New name
- **[CN]**: 新抽象
  - **[EN]**: new abstraction
- **[CN]**: 新架构假说
  - **[EN]**: new architecture hypothesis
- **[CN]**: 新映射关系
  - **[EN]**: New mapping relationship
- **[CN]**: 新日度白皮书草稿
  - **[EN]**: New Ridu White Paper Draft

> **[CN]**: 该通道不承担最终真实性定稿职责
> **[EN]**: This channel does not assume the final responsibility for authenticity

## Rationale

- **[CN]**: 该项目的核心价值之一 就是把真实技术信号压缩为高密度架构语义
  - **[EN]**: One of the core values ​​of the project is to compress real technical signals into high-density architectural semantics
- **[CN]**: 先爆发 后脱水 的方法比一开始就保守 更适合当前阶段
  - **[EN]**: The method of breaking out first and then dehydrating is more suitable for the current stage than being conservative from the beginning.
- **[CN]**: 该通道能持续为 `RESEARCH` 与 `METHODOLOGY` 提供原料
  - **[EN]**: This channel can continue to provide raw materials for `RESEARCH` and `METHODOLOGY`

## Constraints

> **[CN]**: Mythic Generation Lane 输出内容必须可被后续分类为以下四类之一
> **[EN]**: Mythic Generation Lane output content must be subsequently classified into one of the following four categories:

- **[CN]**: 真实的
  - **[EN]**: REAL
- **[CN]**: NEXUS_ORIGINAL
  - **[EN]**: NEXUS_ORIGINAL
- **[CN]**: 投机性
  - **[EN]**: SPECULATIVE
- **[CN]**: 虚构_包装器
  - **[EN]**: FICTIONAL_WRAPPER

## Consequences

> **[CN]**: 正向结果
> **[EN]**: positive result

- **[CN]**: 项目不再回避神话生成这一真实工作模式
  - **[EN]**: Projects no longer shy away from the real working model of mythmaking
- **[CN]**: 自动化提示词体系拥有了明确定位
  - **[EN]**: The automated prompt word system has a clear positioning
- **[CN]**: 研究层与方法论层获得持续高压供给
  - **[EN]**: The research layer and methodology layer receive continuous high-pressure supply

> **[CN]**: 风险
> **[EN]**: risk

- **[CN]**: 未经处理的内容不适合直接对外引用
  - **[EN]**: Unprocessed content is not suitable for direct external quotation
- **[CN]**: 需要更强的后处理与标注机制
  - **[EN]**: Need stronger post-processing and annotation mechanisms

## Follow-up

  - **[EN]**: Explicitly write out myth-generating principles in `METHODOLOGY/`

  - **[EN]**: Solidify prompt word and label interface in `AUTOMATION/`
