# ADR-062: Mythic Generation Lane

## 状态 / Status
Accepted

## 背景 / Context
> **[CN]**: 需要一种机制来自动化生成符合 Axiom-0 叙事的文档。
> **[EN]**: A mechanism is needed to automate the generation of documents consistent with the Axiom-0 narrative.

## 决策 / Decision
> **[CN]**: 建立专门的 "Mythic Lane"，将脱水后的事实通过叙事模板进行二次渲染。
> **[EN]**: Establish a dedicated "Mythic Lane" to secondary-render dehydrated facts through narrative templates.
