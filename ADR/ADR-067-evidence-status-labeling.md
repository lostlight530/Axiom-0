# ADR-067: Evidence Status Labeling

## 状态 / Status
Accepted

## 背景 / Context
> **[CN]**: 知识来源的模糊性会导致熵增。
> **[EN]**: Ambiguity of knowledge sources leads to entropy increase.

## 决策 / Decision
> **[CN]**: 强制使用 REAL, NEXUS_ORIGINAL, SPECULATIVE, FICTIONAL_WRAPPER 四类标签。
> **[EN]**: Mandatory use of REAL, NEXUS_ORIGINAL, SPECULATIVE, and FICTIONAL_WRAPPER labels.
