# ADR-069: Repo Knowledge Stratification

## 状态 / Status
Accepted

## 背景 / Context
> **[CN]**: 知识密度分布不均。
> **[EN]**: Uneven distribution of knowledge density.

## 决策 / Decision
> **[CN]**: 严格遵守研究、方法论、ADR、代码四层结构，禁止跨层污染。
> **[EN]**: Strictly adhere to the four-layer structure (Research, Methodology, ADR, Code), prohibiting cross-layer contamination.
