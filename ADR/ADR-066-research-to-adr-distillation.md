# ADR-066: Research to ADR Distillation

## 状态 / Status
Accepted

## 背景 / Context
> **[CN]**: 研究资产到架构决策的转换路径不清晰。
> **[EN]**: The transformation path from research assets to architectural decisions is unclear.

## 决策 / Decision
> **[CN]**: 每周执行一次“蒸馏循环”，将白皮书中的决议草案提炼为正式 ADR。
> **[EN]**: Execute a "distillation loop" weekly to refine resolution drafts from whitepapers into formal ADRs.
