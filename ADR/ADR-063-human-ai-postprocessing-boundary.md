# ADR-063: Human-AI Postprocessing Boundary

## 状态 / Status
Accepted

## 背景 / Context
> **[CN]**: 纯 AI 生成的内容往往带有极高的高熵噪声。
> **[EN]**: Pure AI-generated content often carries extremely high-entropy noise.

## 决策 / Decision
> **[CN]**: 所有入库资产必须经过人类逻辑锚点（Jules 或 lostlight）的最后校验。
> **[EN]**: All assets entering the repository must pass final verification by human logical anchors (Jules or lostlight).
