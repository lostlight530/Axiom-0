# ADR-004: 液态知识固化法则 / Liquid Knowledge Solidification Law

## 状态 / Status
> **[CN]:** 绝对法则 (Absolute Law)
> **[EN]:** Absolute Law

## 背景 / Context
> **[CN]:** 未经处理的对话、研究草稿以及临时搜索结果在系统中呈现高熵的“液态”甚至“气态”。它们虽然包含有价值的信号，但缺乏拓扑维度的结构刚性，极易随时间流逝和上下文刷新而蒸发，导致整个认知系统的熵增和资源浪费。
> **[EN]:** Unprocessed dialogues, research drafts, and temporary search results appear in the system as high-entropy "liquid" or even "gaseous" states. While containing valuable signals, they lack the structural rigidity of topological dimensions and are highly prone to evaporating over time and with context refreshes, leading to entropy increase and resource waste in the cognitive system.

## 决策 / Decision
> **[CN]:** 系统必须在 T-04 与 T-10 节点之间执行严格的状态相变约束。所有进入液态阶段的临时记忆，必须通过去重、重构与抽象化，最终“固化”为完全确定性的结构化文档。固化后的文件如同冷凝的晶体，成为不可篡改的系统共识，作为未来所有零熵推演的绝对物理基石。
> **[EN]:** The system must enforce strict state phase transition constraints between nodes T-04 and T-10. All temporary memories entering the liquid phase must undergo deduplication, restructuring, and abstraction, ultimately "solidifying" into completely deterministic structured documents. Solidified files, like condensed crystals, become immutable systemic consensus, serving as the absolute physical cornerstones for all future zero-entropy deductions.
