# ADR-004: 液态知识的物理固化 / Physical Solidification of Liquid Knowledge

## 状态 / Status
> **[CN]:** 绝对法则 (Absolute Law)
> **[EN]:** Absolute Law

## 背景 / Context
> **[CN]:** 信息的价值不在于堆砌的体积，而在于其约束力量。如果输入流以散漫的纯文本形式存在，它们将永远只是“液态”的杂音，无法为系统提供工业级的确定性指引，导致知识无法真正沉淀。
> **[EN]:** The value of information lies not in its amassed volume, but in its constraining power. If input streams exist merely as scattered plaintext, they remain "liquid" noise, failing to provide industrial-grade deterministic guidance for the system, thus preventing true knowledge sedimentation.

## 决策 / Decision
> **[CN]:** Axiom-0 规定，日常摄取的高价值信息流必须经过 10 节点连续体的多层严酷蒸馏。最终，它们将被强行凝固成带有不可篡改密码学签名的 ADR (架构决策记录)。一切外部吸收的情报，必须完成到“约束法则”的拓扑转换，实现物理层面的闭环迭代。
> **[EN]:** Axiom-0 dictates that high-value daily ingested information streams must undergo rigorous multi-layer distillation through the 10-node continuum. Ultimately, they must be forcefully solidified into Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) stamped with immutable cryptographic signatures. All absorbed external intelligence must complete a topological transformation into "constraining laws," achieving a closed-loop iteration at the physical level.
