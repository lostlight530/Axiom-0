# ADR-006: 神话学生成通道法则 / Mythic Generation Lane Law

## 状态 / Status
> **[CN]:** 绝对法则 (Absolute Law)
> **[EN]:** Absolute Law

## 背景 / Context
> **[CN]:** 虽然 Axiom-0 拒绝概率与高熵，但人类语言的演进需要隐喻和神话学视角的宏大叙事来传递降维打击的技术哲学。然而，若将这种修辞混入核心执行逻辑，将引发毁灭性的系统崩溃。
> **[EN]:** Although Axiom-0 rejects probability and high entropy, the evolution of human language requires metaphors and grandiose narratives from a mythic perspective to convey the technical philosophy of dimensional reduction strikes. However, mixing such rhetoric into core execution logic will trigger devastating systemic collapses.

## 决策 / Decision
> **[CN]:** 建立隔离的“神话学生成通道”。在输出人类可读的架构文档（如 ADR、白皮书）时，允许引擎在严格的 T-10 物理隔离区内使用冷峻、宏大、历史视角的语言。但该通道的数据流动是单向的，绝不允许这种叙事文本回流参与任何测试期算力（Test-Time Compute）和逻辑流转。
> **[EN]:** Establish an isolated "Mythic Generation Lane." When outputting human-readable architectural documents (e.g., ADRs, whitepapers), the engine is permitted to use cold, grandiose, historically-perspectived language within the strictly physically isolated T-10 zone. However, data flow in this lane is strictly unidirectional; this narrative text is absolutely forbidden from flowing back to participate in any Test-Time Compute or logic transitions.
